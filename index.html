About Me

I am an Application & Cloud Security Specialist II at Bristol Myers Squibb with 5+ years of cybersecurity experience. I am involved in driving and expanding the Application Security program, working with engineering teams to identify vulnerabilities, understand risks, prioritize findings, drive remediation, and improve secure development practices.

My focus goes beyond traditional vulnerability discovery. I am interested in security engineering, automation, Agentic AI, AI security, and finding practical ways to close gaps in security workflows at scale.

  • Application Security: AppSec, vulnerability management, developer enablement, and secure development workflows.
  • Cloud Security: Security across AWS, identity, infrastructure, and cloud-native workloads.
  • Security Engineering: Threat modeling, security architecture, CI/CD security, and Secure SDLC.
  • Automation and AI: Exploring automated workflows and Agentic AI that can assist security teams and developers.
  • Security Innovation: Identifying gaps in existing capabilities and exploring practical solutions to close them.
